## 🎯 Examination Scheme & Preparation Guide
1. **In-Semester (Insem) Exam:** Focuses on **Unit 1 and Unit 2** (typically 30 Marks). Use the specialized `Question_Banks_and_Insem_Prep/` folders containing college-curated unit question banks from PVG's COET, SKN Sinhgad, and MET College.
2. **End-Semester (Endsem) Exam:** Written theory examination of 60 marks (2024 Pattern) covering all 5-6 units.
3. **Solved Papers & Answers:** Dedicated solved question papers are available in `OOP & CG/Insem_and_Solved_Papers/` for Computer Graphics and OOP to guide answer structuring according to SPPU marking rubrics.
