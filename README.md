## Study Guide Features

- **100% SPPU 2024 Pattern Syllabus Coverage:** Written strictly against the official SPPU Revised 2024 syllabus with zero off-syllabus filler.
- **Custom Inline SVG Diagrams & Waveforms:** High-precision schematics, Logic/CMOS/TTL circuits, aligned digital timing waveforms, memory-box & pointer layouts, pixel-trace grids, Gantt charts, and process/memory architectures on light-paper containers readable in Light, Warm, and Dark modes.
- **One-Page Short Notes per Unit:** Printable single-page revision cheat sheets at the end of every unit (`Print 1-Page Sheet`).
- **Interactive Active-Recall Flashcards:** Filter by unit, shuffle, flip, and persist mastered cards per local student profile.
- **Exam Strategy & Solved Papers:** Subject-specific hall time budgets, unit weightage tactics, and full solved SPPU Oct 2025 papers (`[6582]-236` through `[6582]-240`) + Model Digital Marketing Paper.
- **Multi-User Local Tracking (`localStorage`):** Topic completion checkboxes, confidence ratings (`Unseen`, `Revise`, `Good`, `Mastered`), bookmarks, daily mood log, study focus timer, revision roadmap, and JSON backup/restore.
